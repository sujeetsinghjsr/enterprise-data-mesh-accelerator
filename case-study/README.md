# Energy Trading Domain Onboarding

## Executive Summary

This case study demonstrates how a new Energy Trading business domain can onboard to an enterprise Data Mesh platform.

The focus is not only building pipelines.

The focus is enabling an entire business domain through standardized platform engineering, governance, and reusable data products.

---

## Business Context

The Energy Trading domain executes thousands of commodity trades every day.

Multiple downstream teams require trusted datasets.

- Finance
- Risk
- Analytics
- Executive Reporting

Instead of building independent pipelines, the domain adopts a shared enterprise platform.
